Numerical probes

Seven results from openai/math, computed

The collection comprises 722 manuscripts in 372 families, produced by an internal OpenAI model and released at varying stages of verification. The seven families treated here are those, among the 372 surveyed, whose central quantities admit direct evaluation. Each panel plots the quantity its theorem constrains against the value the theorem predicts.

These computations do not verify any proof. An agreement with a stated asymptotic law is evidence about the statement and not about the argument; a disagreement admits two readings, of which an error in the manuscript is the less likely.

Source: github.com/eigendash/openai-math-visualised. The series are regenerated by scripts/build_site_data.py. Static figures, the numerical package, and the test suite are in the same repository; the accompanying note states each theorem, the computation performed, and what the result does and does not establish.

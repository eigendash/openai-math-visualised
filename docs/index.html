Numerical probes

Seven results from openai/math, checked by computation

openai/math is a collection of 722 mathematical manuscripts, written by an internal OpenAI model and released at different stages of verification. They are grouped into 372 families and cover number theory, algebraic geometry, analysis and combinatorics. Most of the results concern objects that cannot be computed numerically at all. These seven can, and each panel plots the quantity its theorem is about against the value the theorem predicts.

None of this verifies a proof. Agreeing with a stated asymptotic law is evidence about the statement, not about the argument, and a disagreement is more likely to be a finite-range effect than an error in the manuscript.

Source: github.com/eigendash/openai-math-visualised. The series are regenerated by scripts/build_site_data.py. Static figures, the numerical package, and the test suite are in the same repository; the accompanying note states each theorem, the computation performed, and what the result does and does not establish.
